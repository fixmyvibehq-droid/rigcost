How much does an Xbox Series X cost to run?

The Xbox Series X uses about 154 W while gaming. At the UK price cap of 26.32p per kWh, that is about 4.1p an hour, or around £44 a year if you play 3 hours a day. These figures are for the console only; your TV adds to it.

Where the figure comes from

Microsoft's own lab measured an average of about 154 W during gameplay across more than 200 games. Real use changes with each game, and menus, streaming apps and downloads use less.

What about the TV?

A TV adds its own power use on top, which varies a lot with size and type. Check the energy label on your TV for its on-mode power in watts and add it to the console's figure.

Compared with a gaming PC

A mid-range gaming PC with an RTX 4070 and a 27-inch monitor uses about 330 W while gaming, about 8.7p an hour. See every console next to a gaming PC or work out your own PC's cost.
